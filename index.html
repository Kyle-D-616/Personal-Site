About Me

Hello! I’m Kyle Dukes, a dedicated Software Developer and Data Engineer with a deep passion for technology and learning.

With over 17 years of diverse professional experience, including 5 years in managerial roles across various industries, I’ve cultivated a broad skill set that I now apply to software and data engineering. While my career path may have seemed eclectic at times, every step has led me to my true calling: coding. These varied experiences uniquely position me to adapt quickly, solve complex problems, and thrive in dynamic environments.



I am entirely self-taught, with formal education limited to general studies. My journey has sharpened my abilities in time management, workflow optimization, research, and analytical problem-solving. I’ve developed a deep understanding of how to learn and what to learn, which has been invaluable in my growth as a technologist. This self-directed learning, paired with mentorship and hands-on experience, has fueled my passion and resilience in the field of technology.



For the past four years, I’ve been immersed in development, including over two years at a smaller company where I wore multiple hats as a data engineer, data scientist, and software engineer. I worked extensively with SQL in Microsoft SQL Server for ETL processes and developed solutions in Python, C#, HTML, and CSS while collaborating with stakeholders to gather requirements and deliver accessible, data-driven insights.



Over the last year, I’ve focused on building data-driven applications and platforms in a large-scale enterprise environment. My work has been primarily on the backend, developing scalable systems to process, transform, and manage data efficiently. In addition to backend development, I’ve contributed to API integration projects and implemented automation solutions to streamline workflows and improve system interoperability.
